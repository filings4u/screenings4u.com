         INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
                                Mobile Testing • On-Site Testing • Testing Events

Effective date: January 1, 2024 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. PARTIES; PURPOSE; DEFINITIONS
This Independent Contractor Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into by screenings4u, LLC, operating
its independent contractor network as “screenings4u | IC Network” (“Company”), with its principal headquarters at
8537 S Pulaski Rd, Chicago, IL 60652, and the independent business or individual identified on the signature page
(“Contractor”). The Agreement begins on the effective date stated above or, if blank, the date of last signature.

The Company arranges or coordinates drug and alcohol testing services for employers and other customers.
Contractor may accept discrete service assignments (“Assignments”) to perform collection and related field services
within Contractor’s verified qualifications. This Agreement is a framework; each accepted Assignment specifies its
operational scope, site, schedule, requested testing type, and any approved fees.

2. AUTHORIZED SERVICES; SCOPE
Only services expressly authorized in an accepted Assignment may be performed. Depending on documented
qualifications and authorization, such services may include: DOT urine specimen collections; authorized DOT oral-
fluid collections where legally and operationally available; non-DOT urine, oral-fluid, hair, and rapid screening
collections; breath alcohol screening/confirmation by properly qualified personnel; mobile and on-site employer
collections; multi-donor testing events; post-accident and reasonable-suspicion collections; and necessary specimen
packaging, custody documentation, shipment coordination, and event reconciliation.

Clinical procedures, venipuncture/phlebotomy, medical examinations, specimen analysis, medical review, diagnosis,
clinical interpretation, and employer compliance decisions are excluded unless separately authorized in writing and
Contractor independently possesses all required licensure, credentials, insurance, and permissions. An Assignment
does not itself confer authorization to conduct a regulated testing method.

3. INDEPENDENT BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP
Contractor operates an independently established business and is engaged as an independent contractor—not as
an employee, partner, joint venturer, franchisee, or general agent of Company. Contractor controls the lawful
business means and methods used to deliver accepted services, subject to objective customer specifications, health
and safety requirements, confidentiality safeguards, manufacturer instructions, chain-of-custody rules, and
applicable laws. Company may audit outcomes, reject nonconforming work, and insist on legally required
procedures without intending to direct Contractor’s unrelated business operations.

Contractor may market services to and work for other customers, including competitors, provided Contractor avoids
actual conflicts of interest and protects Company and customer confidential information. Contractor is not
guaranteed assignments, a schedule, any minimum volume, or an exclusive territory. Contractor may accept or
decline proposed Assignments without penalty unrelated to prior commitments. Once Contractor accepts an
Assignment, Contractor must fulfill it or arrange a Company-approved qualified replacement as provided below.

The parties recognize that worker classification depends on actual facts and applicable law, not merely this
description. No provision waives rights that cannot lawfully be waived; the parties will cooperate in making
necessary prospective adjustments if classification requirements apply.

4. ASSIGNMENT ACCEPTANCE AND EVENT COORDINATION
Company will provide available Assignment details, including customer site, authorized scope, expected headcount,
testing classification (DOT or non-DOT), reporting contact, appointment window, supplies responsibility, and rates
under Exhibit A or written Assignment terms. Contractor must promptly disclose scheduling conflicts, geographic or
equipment limitations, and qualification gaps before acceptance.

For accepted work, Contractor will coordinate professional arrival, site readiness, donor flow, safety, identification,
privacy, and secure specimen handling. Contractor must promptly inform the designated Company coordinator of
material delays, cancellations, incidents, refusals, collection problems, insufficient supplies, inaccessible sites, and
potential violations. Changes in scope or charges require written authorization before Contractor incurs them,
except immediate steps necessary to protect donor safety or specimen integrity.

For multi-donor testing events, Contractor shall reconcile attendees, completed tests, incomplete or refused tests,
specimen shipment records, custody forms, and supplies used; Contractor shall not guarantee a specific number of
completed collections.

5. QUALIFICATIONS; TRAINING; CONTINUING ELIGIBILITY
Before accepting any particular service, Contractor must provide authentic and current evidence of required
qualifications, including, where applicable, DOT urine collector training and proficiency demonstrations, oral-fluid
collector qualifications, Breath Alcohol Technician (BAT) and/or Screening Test Technician (STT) credentials, device-
specific proficiency, state-required credentials, occupational exposure training, and applicable continuing or error-
correction training. Contractor shall not misrepresent certification, licensure, experience, or ability.

Contractor must maintain credentials and training records, follow changes in governing regulations and procedures,
and immediately notify Company of suspension, expiration, restriction, investigation affecting eligibility, or a
material collection error. Company may verify qualifications and suspend dispatch of Assignments pending
satisfactory documentation. Optional Company orientation addresses assignment logistics and security
requirements; it does not replace legally required qualification or transform Contractor into an employee.

6. DOT / NON-DOT QUALITY AND CHAIN OF CUSTODY
For DOT-regulated work, Contractor shall comply with all applicable requirements of 49 CFR Part 40, the relevant
DOT-agency rules, current federal custody-and-control forms, approved devices, laboratories, collection procedures,
and applicable DOT guidance. Contractor may perform only the DOT functions for which qualified, must preserve
specimen integrity and required privacy, document and correct issues when permitted, and never substitute non-
DOT protocols or forms for DOT-required procedures.

For non-DOT work, Contractor shall follow the accepted Assignment, lawful employer-authority program
requirements, applicable state/local law, consent and authorization rules, device instructions, approved forms,
laboratory procedures, and applicable chain-of-custody practices. DOT and non-DOT samples, records, and reporting
workflows must be clearly distinguished. Contractor must not independently select panels, change donor eligibility,
make fitness-for-duty determinations, or communicate final drug-test interpretations beyond the authority of the
collector role.

No collection shall be fabricated, backdated, knowingly mislabeled, altered, or performed on behalf of a donor.
Contractor must record and escalate refusals, unusual behavior, suspected adulteration or substitution, insufficient
specimens, equipment failures, temperature irregularities, and other procedural issues accurately and promptly.

7. MOBILE / ON-SITE SAFETY; EQUIPMENT AND SUPPLIES
Contractor shall provide a safe, clean, orderly collection environment meeting applicable privacy, sanitation, site
security, access, infection-control, and occupational safety standards; use appropriate personal protective
equipment and approved disposal procedures; and maintain any needed professional equipment, calibration,

quality-control and maintenance records. Contractor must not use unapproved collection supplies, expired devices,
or an unqualified substitute.

Unless an Assignment expressly states otherwise, Contractor supplies ordinary business tools, transportation,
mobile devices, connectivity, and routine operating costs. Test kits, specialized equipment, laboratory shipments,
and branded materials are allocated by each Assignment. Contractor will inventory and safeguard Company- or
client-provided materials and promptly return unused supplies as directed.

Contractor shall comply with reasonable site-entry rules, applicable workplace safety policies, and incident reporting
procedures; such requirements do not authorize Company to control Contractor’s unrelated business activities.

8. PERSONNEL; SUBCONTRACTING
Contractor may use personnel only with Company’s prior written approval for a specific Assignment or standing
approved roster. Each person must independently meet the qualifications, screening, privacy, and insurance
requirements for the work performed and execute any required confidentiality or data-access commitments.
Contractor is responsible for its personnel, their compensation, payroll and tax obligations, and their acts and
omissions. No delegation may impair chain-of-custody integrity or allow unauthorized portal access.

9. COMPENSATION; INVOICES; EXPENSES
Compensation is determined solely by completed Exhibit A or an accepted written Assignment. ALL RATES,
SURCHARGES, EVENT MINIMUMS, CANCELLATION TERMS AND PAYMENT TIMING ARE LEFT BLANK FOR THE PARTIES
TO COMPLETE. The Company owes no payment for unaccepted work, duplicate invoices, fabricated work, or services
materially outside the authorized scope. Legitimate disputed amounts will be addressed in good faith without
withholding undisputed amounts contrary to applicable law.

Contractor shall submit accurate itemized invoices or approved event completion records showing Assignment
identifier, service date/location, authorized service types, number of completed collections, approved extras, and
supporting records without including unnecessary donor health information. Contractor bears its own taxes,
licensing, business expenses, and required insurance unless an Assignment expressly specifies a separately
approved reimbursement. Company will issue information returns when legally required; tax reporting form or
treatment does not itself determine worker classification.

10. NO AUTHORITY TO BIND COMPANY; CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
Contractor may not sign contracts on Company’s behalf, modify customer prices, represent itself as a Company
employee, offer medical advice, promise result turnaround times, alter customer testing policies, collect
unauthorized fees from donors, or make commitments beyond the accepted Assignment. Contractor may accurately
describe itself as an independent collection provider assigned through screenings4u | IC Network.

Contractor shall route service disputes, complaints, requests for changes, and requests for protected results
through designated channels and shall not solicit or redirect assigned donors into off-platform transactions using
confidential customer information.

11. PRIVACY, SECURITY, AND CONFIDENTIALITY
“Protected Information” includes personal identifiers, donor information, employer lists, testing orders, specimen
and custody records, test status or results, credentials, private portal content, business records, nonpublic rates,
security information, and other confidential data. Contractor may access and use Protected Information only as
necessary for an authorized Assignment; may disclose it only to authorized persons, laboratories, medical review
personnel, Company contacts, or regulators when legally permitted; and shall follow all applicable privacy, data-
protection, and recordkeeping requirements, including HIPAA only where and to the extent actually applicable.

Contractor must use authorized systems and individual credentials; restrict devices through screen lock and current
security updates; protect papers and specimens in transit; avoid unapproved personal email, messaging, cloud
storage, photography, and removable media; and never send test results or sensitive identifiers through public
website contact forms. Contractor must notify Company without undue delay—and, for a suspected loss,
compromise, or unauthorized access, no later than 24 hours after discovery—at icnetwork@screenings4u.com,
preserve evidence, cooperate in containment and legally required notifications, and not notify affected parties
independently unless required by law or approved by Company.

Contractor shall return or securely destroy Company and customer information on request or termination, except
records retained under an applicable legal requirement; retained materials remain protected. Confidentiality
survives termination for as long as information remains confidential or law requires, and trade-secret obligations
survive while protected by law. Nothing prevents protected reporting to government agencies, regulators, counsel,
or legally protected whistleblowing.

12. PORTALS; RECORDS; AUDIT COOPERATION
When granted access, Contractor shall use designated screenings4u customer, Workforce DOT, or non-DOT
workflow tools only within assigned permissions; maintain accurate and timely collection and event records; correct
errors transparently without deleting audit trails; and promptly provide records legitimately required for customer,
laboratory, regulatory, insurance, or quality reviews. No unverified website description expands Contractor’s access
rights.

Company may review collection outcomes, training credentials, invoices, event reconciliation, custody
documentation, and reasonable security evidence connected with assigned services, with due respect for
Contractor’s independent operations and third-party confidentiality obligations.

13. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT; CONFLICTS; SAFETY INCIDENTS
Contractor shall treat donors fairly, protect donor dignity, maintain appropriate attire and hygiene for testing
environments, avoid harassment, discrimination, retaliation, intoxication or impairment while performing services,
and comply with applicable anti-discrimination, safety, and accessibility laws. Contractor shall promptly disclose
actual conflicts of interest that could undermine a collection, including any prohibited collector/donor relationship
under DOT rules.

Company may decline to assign or may remove Contractor from an Assignment where objectively necessary to
protect safety, privacy, lawful compliance, customer property, or specimen integrity. No general right of day-to-day
personnel supervision is created.

14. INSURANCE; INDEMNIFICATION; LIABILITY
Before performing assignments, Contractor shall maintain appropriate coverage for its operations, including
automobile liability for vehicles used, professional liability/errors and omissions, and commercial general liability
where appropriate; any minimum coverage limits, additional-insured endorsement requirements, and specific
certificates shall be identified in Exhibit B or the relevant Assignment and are subject to insurer availability and policy
terms. Workers’ compensation or occupational accident coverage shall be maintained where required by applicable
law.

To the extent permitted by law, Contractor shall indemnify Company against third-party claims, liabilities, damages
and reasonable defense costs arising from Contractor’s negligent or wrongful acts, willful misconduct, material
breach, or violations of law, except to the extent caused by Company’s negligence or wrongful conduct. Company
shall be responsible for claims to the extent caused by its own negligence or wrongful conduct. Neither party waives
statutory rights that cannot be waived. Neither party is excused from responsibility for confidentiality breaches,
fraud, or intentional misconduct by a contractual limitation.

15. TAXES; BUSINESS COMPLIANCE; NO EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
Contractor is responsible for operating registrations, tax filings, licenses, permitted personnel, insurance and
business expenses; Company will not provide employee benefits, paid leave, or payroll tax withholding unless
required by law. Contractor shall furnish a completed Form W-9 or appropriate tax documentation before payment.
Contractor shall not hold itself out as eligible for employee benefits merely by accepting Assignments.

16. TERM; SUSPENSION; TERMINATION
This Agreement continues until terminated. Either party may terminate on thirty (30) days’ written notice. Either
party may terminate immediately for material breach, loss of qualifications, serious safety or privacy risk, fraud,
repeated documentation failures, or unlawful conduct. Company may suspend future Assignments while a reported
issue is investigated. The parties will coordinate lawful disposition of already accepted Assignments; completed,
conforming services remain payable under applicable terms.

On termination, Contractor shall cease representing an affiliation, return property and credentials, deliver
outstanding collection and custody records, and comply with privacy retention and destruction obligations. Clauses
intended by nature to survive, including payment reconciliation, confidentiality, liability, disputes, and record
obligations, remain effective.

17. NON-EXCLUSIVITY; NO IMPROPER RESTRICTIONS
This Agreement does not prohibit Contractor from working for other entities, advertising independent services, or
maintaining an independent customer base. Contractor may not misuse Protected Information, misrepresent
Company affiliation, or interfere with an accepted Assignment. No blanket noncompetition restriction is imposed.

18. NOTICES; GOVERNING LAW; DISPUTES
Official notices to Company shall be addressed to screenings4u | IC Network, 8537 S Pulaski Rd, Chicago, IL 60652,
email icnetwork@screenings4u.com. Notices to Contractor shall use the contact information on the signature page.
Routine notices may be sent by email with reasonable evidence of transmission; termination and breach notices
should additionally be sent by tracked delivery or acknowledged email.

Illinois law governs, excluding choice-of-law rules, subject to overriding federal law and nonwaivable protections of
another applicable jurisdiction. The parties will first attempt a good-faith business resolution. Unless mandatory law
requires otherwise, disputes shall be brought in state or federal courts with proper jurisdiction in Cook County,
Illinois. Nothing prevents filing a report or claim with an agency authorized to investigate worker status, safety, or
testing compliance.

19. GENERAL PROVISIONS
This Agreement and its signed exhibits and accepted Assignment documents form the agreement regarding the
subject matter and supersede prior agreements only as to work covered here. Assignment-specific scope and pricing
govern the corresponding Assignment; mandatory legal requirements prevail over conflicting language.
Amendments must be in writing and accepted by both parties. No waiver of one breach waives another. Invalid
provisions shall be limited or severed without invalidating the remainder. Neither party may assign this Agreement
without written consent, except Company may assign it in connection with a bona fide reorganization or sale subject
to continuation of applicable obligations. Counterparts and electronic signatures are permitted to the extent legally
valid.

The parties acknowledge the opportunity to consult independent legal and tax advisors and confirm that each
person signing possesses authority to bind the identified party.
