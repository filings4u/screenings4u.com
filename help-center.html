How do I order a drug or alcohol test?+
Choose a test from the testing catalog, review its details, and follow the secure checkout instructions. If the service needs additional donor information or a collection location, complete those steps before visiting a site. Retain your order confirmation for reference.

How can I review an order I already placed?+
Sign in to the customer portal using the account associated with your purchase. Check the order details and any available authorizations. If you cannot find an order, contact support with the order reference and the email used to order; do not email sensitive health details.

Can I purchase tests for multiple people?+
Each person must have accurate donor information and an appropriate authorization. The current checkout flow is designed around individual tests; do not assume that placing multiple items into a cart completes donor registration for everyone. For employer or group arrangements, contact our team for the correct process.

What happens if a payment or checkout step fails?+
First, check whether you received a successful order confirmation or an order in your account. Avoid repeated purchases until you know whether the original payment completed. If you need assistance, contact support and include the order reference if available; do not share full payment-card numbers.

What is a donor pass?+
A donor pass or collection authorization helps the collection site identify the appropriate test order. Depending on the service, it may include collection instructions and location details. Only use the pass issued for the correct person and order.
